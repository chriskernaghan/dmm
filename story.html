By a regular (name or initials, as they prefer) · 5 min read · Date to come

A short standfirst goes here, in a sentence or two: what the piece is about and why a stranger might want to read it.

The body of a story is plain, readable text with room to breathe. It might start with the first time someone nearly didn't come, move through what the walks were actually like and finish somewhere honest rather than neat, which is how most of us would prefer it.

Longer pieces can carry subheadings, links and the odd photo from the hill. Stories can be edited with their author by either Aidan or Chris, and nothing goes live until the author has seen the final version.

A pull quote can sit here when a line deserves a bit more space.

Announcements use the same template, so a note about winter start times looks like the rest of the journal and sits in the same place.
