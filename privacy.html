Privacy

How we look after your details

We collect as little as we can get away with, we only use it for what you'd expect, and you can ask us to delete it whenever you like.

Who we are

Divis Mountain Men is a free community for men, founded by Aidan Malone, that meets on Divis Mountain. The person responsible for your information is [Aidan Malone / organisation details to confirm]. You can reach us at [contact email to confirm].

What we collect, and why

  • Joining the mailing list. Your email address, so we can send walk details and the occasional story. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
  • Booking a place. Your name, a way to contact you and an emergency contact. Aidan uses these to confirm your place and to reach someone if something goes wrong on a walk. If you give us someone else's details, please check they're happy for you to.
  • Sharing a story. Whatever you send us, plus your name or initials if you want one used. Nothing is published without your agreement, and you can ask for it to be changed or taken down.
  • Supporting the community. Payments are taken by a separate payment provider, [provider to confirm]. We don't see your card details. We do see the amount and the name and email you give, so Aidan can send a receipt and keep the accounts.
  • Visiting the site. [Basic, privacy-friendly visitor statistics, or none at all: to confirm once we decide.]

Who else sees it

We use a few separate tools to do the above: [mailing list tool], [booking tool] and [payment provider]. They handle your information on our behalf and only for those jobs. We don't sell your details or use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

[Mailing list: until you unsubscribe. Bookings and emergency contacts: to confirm, but no longer than needed after the walk. Payment records: as long as the law requires for accounts.]

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can object to us using it. Email us and we'll sort it, usually within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled something, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own at the moment. It does load its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads. The booking, mailing list and payment tools we plan to add may set cookies of their own, and when they're in place we'll list them here and ask first wherever the law says we should.
