Every Tuesday

Turn up as you are.

Come once, come every week, or come and say very little for the first hour. Most of the talking happens side by side, which turns out to be easier than across a table. Dogs are welcome, and some of them have strong opinions.

Book a place

Let Aidan know you're coming.

Walks are free, and a booking just means someone knows to look out for you. Aidan confirms each one personally, so you'll hear back from a real person rather than an automated reply.

  1. Pick the walk you'd like to join and send a request.
  2. Give us a name, a way to reach you and an emergency contact. Only Aidan sees it, and it's there in case something goes wrong on the hill.
  3. Aidan confirms by email, with the meeting point and anything worth bringing.

About

It started with a walk, and mostly still is one.

Divis Mountain Men is a free and inclusive community founded by Aidan Malone. Men of all ages and backgrounds get outside, talk honestly and head home a bit lighter than they arrived.

The community runs on goodwill and the occasional voluntary contribution, which keeps the events free for whoever needs them.
