Support for you

If you're struggling, you don't have to sort it out alone.

You don't need to be at crisis point to ask for help, and you don't need to have been on a walk to use this page.

Draft for Aidan to check. Please confirm the services and the wording before this goes live. Phone numbers and opening hours were checked on 3 November 2026 and are worth re-checking now and then.

In immediate danger?

If you or someone else is at risk right now, call 999 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Call 999

Someone to talk to, any time

These services are free and confidential, and the people on the other end are used to hearing from men who aren't sure what to say.

Northern Ireland

Lifeline

0808 808 8000

Crisis helpline for anyone in Northern Ireland, open 24 hours a day, every day. Textphone: 18001 0808 808 8000. You can also call if you're worried about someone else.

UK and Ireland

Samaritans

116 123

Free from any phone, 24 hours a day, every day. You can talk about anything that's upsetting you, however big or small it seems.

For men

CALM

0800 58 58 58

A helpline and webchat for men in the UK, open from 5pm to midnight every day. Free and anonymous.

By text

Shout

Text SHOUT to 85258

A free text service for anyone who'd rather not talk out loud, open 24 hours a day, every day.

Other places to start

  • Your GP can see you about low mood, anxiety or just not feeling like yourself, and it's worth asking for the earliest appointment.
  • If you're worried about someone else, Lifeline takes calls from friends and family as well as from the person who's struggling.
  • Talk to someone you trust, even if all you manage is "I haven't been great lately". It's often a better opening than it feels.

What Divis Mountain Men is, and isn't

We're a walking community. Plenty of men find that walking and talking helps, but we're not a crisis or counselling service, and the people on the walks aren't there to give clinical advice. If you're in a bad place, please use one of the services above, and if you'd like company on the hill as well, you'll be welcome.
